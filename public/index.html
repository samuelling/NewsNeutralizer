Scores describe the wording of headlines as judged by a language model. They are estimates, not verdicts, and the model makes mistakes in both directions. Rewrites use only the headline and its URL, so they can't add context the article provides. Lean markers: L2/L1 = left-coded wording, R1/R2 = right-coded, · = neutral.
